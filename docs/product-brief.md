# FairLoan — Product Brief

**Version:** 0.3  
**Author:** Vivek Nishad  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Approved for Phase 1 build

---

## One-Line Summary
FairLoan is a transparent digital-lending prototype for India that demonstrates what a responsible, comprehension-first borrower experience should look like.

## Problem
First-time salaried borrowers in India cannot evaluate digital loan offers confidently — not because they lack intelligence, but because lending UX is designed for conversion, not comprehension.

## Evidence (directional — not statistically significant)
- Evidence-backed signal: Cost comprehension is weak (avg 3/5 in sample)
- Evidence-backed signal: Consent screens feel confusing or too long
- Evidence-backed signal: Users feel apps access more data than necessary
- Directional finding: Document requirements create social friction with employers
- Unvalidated hypothesis: Rejection without explanation is disorienting

## Persona
**Priya — research-informed provisional persona**  
27-year-old marketing executive, Lucknow, ₹28,000/month, needs ₹30,000.  
Primary anxiety: "I don't know what I'm agreeing to."

## Product Principles
1. Comprehension before conversion
2. Earn data, don't extract it
3. Rejection is not failure — opacity is
4. No dark patterns, ever
5. Prototype honesty on every screen
6. Borrower protection is a first-class metric

## MVP Scope (Priority Order)
1. "What you'll need" screen
2. Consent + privacy explanation
3. Eligibility pre-check
4. Application flow
5. Transparent loan offer + KFS
6. Decision explanation
7. Cooling-off screen
8. Repayment dashboard (read-only)
9. Support + FAQ (static)

## North Star Metric
**Offer understanding rate** — % of users who can correctly answer "what is my monthly payment and total cost?" after viewing the offer screen.

## What This Is Not
- A real lender
- RBI-compliant
- Suitable for production
- Using real personal data
