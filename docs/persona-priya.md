## What She Needs from FairLoan

> "Tell me what you'll need before I start. Explain why you're asking. Show me exactly what I'll pay. Don't surprise me. And don't make me feel stupid for not knowing."

## What She Is NOT

- A domain expert (R6 was — Priya is not)
- A developer
- Someone who reads T&Cs
- Someone who knows what APR means

## How to Use This Persona

- Every screen design decision: "Would Priya understand this without help?"
- Every copy decision: "Would Priya feel pressured, confused, or surveilled by this?"
- Every error state: "Would Priya know what to do next?"
- Do not design for R6 (domain expert). Design for Priya.
